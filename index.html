Nutrient Plan

A calorie-free approach to personalized nutrition. Focus on what your body needs, not the numbers.

Why We Built This

Project Context: This platform is developed as part of a research project exploring the impact of social media on body image and dietary habits.

We identified several main issues in modern diet culture influenced by social media:

  • Social Media Influence: Constant exposure to idealized body images and fitness posts on social media creates unrealistic standards and body anxiety.
  • Restrictive Diets: Over-fixation on calorie counting and extreme food restriction.
  • Over-exercising: Unhealthy compensatory behaviors driven by guilt.
  • Over Self-Checking: Constant weighing and mirror checking that lead to obsessive thoughts about appearance.

Our Solution: Instead of focusing on calories and weight numbers, we want to help individuals build a healthy relationship with food by understanding nutrients. We provide personalized nutrient targets, encourage using available food in your fridge, and promote a healthy, non-obsessive routine for weight monitoring.
