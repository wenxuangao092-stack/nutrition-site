Before You Start

This is not a diagnosis. It's a gentle check-in to make sure this tool is right for you right now.

1. Do you make yourself sick or skip meals because you feel uncomfortably full or guilty?

2. Do you believe you are fat, even when others tell you that you are too thin?

3. Do you spend more than an hour a day thinking about or checking your body (mirrors, weighing, comparing)?

4. Would you say that food or your weight dominates your life and causes you distress?

If you are struggling, please reach out to a healthcare professional.

Why We Built This

Project Context: This platform is developed as part of a research project exploring the impact of social media on body image and dietary habits.

We identified several main issues in modern diet culture influenced by social media:

  • Social Media Influence: Constant exposure to idealized body images creates unrealistic standards.
  • Restrictive Diets: Over-fixation on calorie counting and extreme restriction.
  • Over-exercising: Unhealthy compensatory behaviors driven by guilt.
  • Over Self-Checking: Constant weighing and mirror checking that lead to obsessive thoughts.

Our Solution: Instead of calories, we help you build a healthy relationship with food through nutrients and how your body actually feels.
