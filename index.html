Why We Built This

Project Context: This platform is developed as part of a research project exploring the impact of social media on body image and dietary habits.

We identified several main issues in modern diet culture influenced by social media:

  • Social Media Influence: Constant exposure to idealized body images and fitness posts creates unrealistic standards.
  • Restrictive Diets: Over-fixation on calorie counting and extreme restriction.
  • Over-exercising: Unhealthy compensatory behaviors driven by guilt.
  • Over Self-Checking: Constant weighing and mirror checking that lead to obsessive thoughts.

Our Solution: Instead of calories, we help you build a healthy relationship with food through nutrients. Personalized targets, fridge-based cooking, and a non-obsessive routine.
